## 1. Introduction & Scope
This Privacy Policy describes how **Word Mapping** ("we", "our", or "the Game"), created and owned by **Rambir**, collects, uses, stores, and protects user information when you play the game through Meta Platforms, Inc. ("Meta", "Facebook", "Messenger").

We are deeply committed to safeguarding player privacy. Word Mapping is designed as an educational, client-side HTML5 puzzle game that prioritizes data minimization, transparent processing, and strict user control.

---

## 2. Information We Collect and Process

### 2.1 Meta Instant Games Scoped Player Data
When you play Word Mapping on Facebook or Messenger, the game connects directly to the official Facebook Instant Games JavaScript SDK (`FBInstant`). With your permission, the game retrieves minimal, platform-scoped identifiers:
- **Game-Scoped Player ID (`FBInstant.player.getID()`):** An anonymized, application-specific alphanumeric identifier generated by Meta. This ID is unique to Word Mapping and cannot be used to identify you across external applications or websites.
- **Player Name / Profile Name (`FBInstant.player.getName()`):** Your public Facebook first name or display alias, utilized exclusively within the game client to personalize in-game greetings, level completion banners, and localized tournament leaderboards.
- **Player Profile Photo (`FBInstant.player.getPhoto()`):** Your public profile thumbnail, processed ephemerally in-memory to display your avatar on leaderboard rankings.

### 2.2 Game Progress and Economy State
To ensure your gameplay state is preserved across sessions and devices, the game stores:
- Current level progress (Levels 1 through 20).
- In-game virtual currency balance (coins).
- Bonus word discoveries and vocabulary translation unlocks.
- Audio and user preference settings (sound effects, music, visual preferences).

### 2.3 Local Storage Fallback (Unauthenticated / Solo Sessions)
If you play the game in an unauthenticated session, solo sandbox mode, or if third-party cookies/storage restrictions prevent Meta player authentication, **Word Mapping** operates entirely locally. In this scenario:
- No network requests are made to save player identifiers.
- Game progress and coin balances are saved locally to your device's browser using HTML5 `window.localStorage`.
- This data remains strictly on your local device and is never transmitted to any external server.

### 2.4 Data We Do NOT Collect
Word Mapping does **NOT** collect, request, access, or store:
- Personal email addresses, physical mailing addresses, or phone numbers.
- Passwords or account credentials.
- Financial, credit card, or payment billing information.
- Precise GPS location data or biometric identifiers.
- Private messages, contacts, or social graph connections.

## 3. How We Use Player Information
All processed data is used strictly for legitimate gameplay operations:
1. **Cloud Progress Synchronization:** Utilizing `FBInstant.player.setDataAsync()` to sync your 20-level progress and coin balances to Meta's secure cloud infrastructure.
2. **Leaderboards & Context Tournaments:** Utilizing `FBInstant.getLeaderboardAsync()` to show your position among friends or tournament participants.
3. **Gameplay Personalization:** Presenting your display name and avatar during level victory celebrations.

---

## 4. Third-Party Sharing and Data Transmission
- **Zero External Server Transmission:** Word Mapping does **NOT** operate any external proprietary servers or third-party tracking databases. Player IDs, scores, and save data are never sent to external third parties.
- **No Sale of Personal Data:** We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade player data to data brokers, advertising agencies, or marketing companies.
- **Meta Ecosystem Containment:** All network communications occur exclusively between your browser client and Meta's official Instant Games API endpoints.

---

## 5. Monetization & Advertising (Facebook Audience Network)
Word Mapping provides free access to all 20 levels. The game is monetized exclusively through rewarded video advertisements provided by Meta:
- **Rewarded Video Ads (`FBInstant.getRewardedVideoAsync()`):** Players may voluntarily watch rewarded video ads to earn bonus virtual coins.
- **Ad Delivery:** Advertisements are served directly by the **Facebook Audience Network** according to Meta's Privacy Policy and your Meta ad preferences.
- We do not integrate any unauthorized third-party ad mediation SDKs, analytics trackers, or banner trackers.

---

## 6. Data Retention and Security
- **Cloud Data:** Save data synchronized via `FBInstant.player.setDataAsync()` is retained within Meta's cloud servers for as long as you maintain Word Mapping in your Facebook account.
- **Local Data:** Data stored in browser `localStorage` persists until you clear your browser cookies, site data, or cache.
- **Security:** All communications with Meta Instant Games APIs are encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS/HTTPS protocols managed by Meta.

## 7. Data Deletion Instructions (User Rights & Deletion Protocol)
In compliance with Meta Platform Terms, GDPR, and global data privacy standards, you have the absolute right to delete all data associated with Word Mapping at any time.

Because Word Mapping stores player data natively within Meta's cloud infrastructure and locally in your browser, you can completely erase your data using the following simple steps:

### Option A: Remove the Game from Facebook (Deletes Cloud Data)
1. Log in to your Facebook account on web or mobile.
2. Navigate to **Settings & Privacy** > **Settings**.
3. In the left-hand menu, select **Apps and Websites** (or **Games & Apps**).
4. Locate **Word Mapping** in your active games list.
5. Click **Remove**.
6. Check the box to confirm: *"Delete posts, videos or events Word Mapping posted on your timeline"* and confirm removal.
7. Upon removing the app, Meta automatically revokes the game-scoped player token and disassociates your profile from the game's cloud save data.

### Option B: Clear Local Browser Data (Deletes Local Storage Data)
1. In your browser settings, go to **Privacy & Security** > **Site Settings**.
2. Select **Cookies and Site Data**.
3. Search for `facebook.com` / `fb.gg` or the host domain.
4. Clear stored local storage data to delete all offline coin balances and level progression.

### Option C: Direct Deletion Request
You may also contact the developer directly to request confirmation of data deletion:
- **Email:** privacy@rambirbhatiwal.com
- **Subject:** Word Mapping Data Deletion Request
- Please provide your Facebook User/Player ID (available in Facebook Settings > Apps and Websites > Word Mapping > View Details). We will respond and confirm deletion within 30 calendar days.
